# Strikefall Support

Need help with Strikefall? Contact us at:

**Email:** [info@auravion.com](mailto:info@auravion.com)

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Your iPhone or iPad model
- Your iOS version
- Your Strikefall version
- A screenshot or description of the problem

## Purchases

Use **Restore Purchases** in the Shop for previously purchased non-consumable items. Never send payment-card or banking information in a support request.

## Advertising

Rewarded ads depend on network connectivity and ad availability. If an ad does not load, check your connection and try again later.

## Privacy

Read the [Strikefall Privacy Policy](https://omran-faour.github.io/strikefall-legal/privacy.html).

We normally respond within three business days.
