Privacy Policy for Strikefall

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Strikefall is a mobile action game developed by Auravion. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you play Strikefall.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: info@auravion.com

Information We Collect

Strikefall does not require users to create an account.

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, country/region, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data.
  • Gameplay and app activity, such as game progress, settings, purchases, virtual currency balances, and interactions with game features.
  • Purchase information, such as product identifiers and transaction status, processed through Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, diagnostics, and ad interaction data used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

How We Use Information

  • Operate and improve the game.
  • Save game progress and settings.
  • Process in-app purchases.
  • Detect crashes, bugs, and performance issues.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Show ads and measure ad performance, if ads are enabled.
  • Comply with legal and platform requirements.

Third-Party Services

Strikefall uses third-party services to operate the game, process purchases, display ads, measure ad performance, and comply with platform requirements.

  • Google Play Billing is used to process in-app purchases. Payment details are handled by Google Play. Strikefall may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and transaction-related information needed to unlock purchased items.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app activity related to ads, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to provide, personalize, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.
  • Google User Messaging Platform may be used to request and manage user consent for ads where required.

For more information about how Google uses data, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the game, process purchases, provide ads, analyze performance, detect crashes, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the game.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. To request deletion of data associated with Strikefall, contact us at info@auravion.com.

Children

Strikefall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
