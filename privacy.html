Privacy Policy for Strikefall Last updated: May 29, 2026 Strikefall is a mobile action game developed by Auravion. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you play Strikefall. Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: info@auravion.com Information We Collect Strikefall does not require users to create an account. Depending on the version of the game and enabled features, Strikefall and third-party service providers may collect or process the following information: - Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, country/region, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data. - Gameplay and app activity, such as game progress, settings, purchases, virtual currency balances, and interactions with game features. - Purchase information, such as product identifiers and transaction status, processed through Google Play Billing. - Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data, if ads are enabled in the game. We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health data, or financial account information. How We Use Information We use information to: - Operate and improve the game. - Save game progress and settings. - Process in-app purchases. - Detect crashes, bugs, and performance issues. - Prevent fraud and abuse. - Show ads, measure ad performance, and manage ad frequency, if ads are enabled. - Comply with legal and platform requirements. Third-Party Services Strikefall may use third-party services that process data according to their own privacy policies, including: - Google Play services and Google Play Billing - Unity services - AppLovin MAX or other advertising mediation services, if ads are enabled - Analytics, crash reporting, or performance monitoring services, if enabled These services may collect device identifiers, diagnostics, app activity, purchase status, advertising identifiers, and related technical data. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the game, process purchases, provide ads, analyze performance, detect crashes, or comply with legal obligations. Data Security We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the game. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect user data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Data Retention and Deletion We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, such as operating the game, maintaining purchase records, preventing fraud, resolving issues, and complying with legal requirements. If you want to request deletion of data associated with Strikefall, contact us at: info@auravion.com Some purchase records may be retained where required for legal, tax, fraud prevention, or platform compliance reasons. Children Strikefall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
