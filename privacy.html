Privacy Policy for Strikefall

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Strikefall is a mobile action game developed by Auravion. This policy describes the game’s accounts, purchases, advertising and your choices. Contact us at info@auravion.com or visit Strikefall support.

Game accounts and saved progress

You can play without registering an email address or creating a password. When online purchase services connect, Unity Authentication creates a guest game account with a generated player identifier. We use this identifier to maintain your purchased-credit wallet and verified ownership. Game progress, earned credits, settings and a cached copy of verified purchases are stored on your device.

You may optionally connect Apple Game Center on iOS, or Google Play Games on Android, to protect and recover your purchased wallet. Unity receives the platform identifier and authentication proof needed to link that account. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. Recovery of the purchased wallet does not upload or restore all local mission progress.

Purchases, balances and refunds

Apple’s App Store or Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. Our server receives purchase proofs, product and transaction identifiers, store environment, purchase and refund status, and an account binding. It verifies these with the store to grant items, restore eligible permanent purchases, prevent duplicate credits, and apply refunds. We store the purchased balance, permanent ownership, credit-spending operations, any refunded-credit shortfall and transaction-deduplication records.

These services run through Unity Gaming Services and our wallet backend hosted on Google Cloud. Store proofs are used for verification; we do not include them in ordinary application logs. Purchases and account recovery require an internet connection. Refund decisions are made by the relevant store.

Advertising and device information

Strikefall uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Depending on your choices and platform permissions, these services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system, app version, language, ad views and interactions, app activity, diagnostics, performance information and purchase-related signals. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, operate their services and, when permitted, personalise advertising.

On iOS, before starting the advertising services, the game offers three choices: personalised ads, basic ads, or no ad permission. Basic ads use limited data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; the game sends a negative personalisation-consent signal and an opt-out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising to the supported ad networks. Personalised ads additionally require your iOS tracking permission. Declining does not prevent gameplay or purchases, but advertising rewards require an available ad.

You can change this choice under Settings → Privacy & terms. Changes stop future ad requests under the previous choice. Close and reopen the game after withdrawing ad permission to fully stop an advertising service already started during that session. iOS tracking permission can also be changed in device Settings. On Android, device advertising settings and the ad provider’s privacy controls are available; the in-game privacy screen described here applies to iOS.

Advertising disclosures and provider choices are available in Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Use the privacy information or choices link within an ad for provider-specific controls and deletion requests. Choosing basic ads or declining ads opts out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising through the game’s supported iOS integrations.

Support and technical information

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send, and use them to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or authentication codes. Our service providers may process technical connection logs and diagnostics to keep authentication, purchases and the game operating securely.

Purposes and sharing

We use information to provide the game and purchases you request, maintain balances and ownership, offer optional recovery, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability. Optional advertising uses your choices and applicable permissions. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the requested service, consent for optional advertising access and personalisation, legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention where permitted, or applicable legal obligations.

We share information needed for these purposes with Unity, Apple or Google as the relevant platform, and Google Cloud as our backend hosting provider. Ad providers may share data with advertising and measurement partners under their policies and your permissions. Processing may take place outside your country; providers describe their international-transfer safeguards in their privacy policies. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and service security.

See Apple’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy and Unity’s privacy policy.

Delete your game account and data

In the game, open Settings → Restore purchases → Delete account, read the consequences, and confirm Delete account and data. An internet connection is required. The game deletes the current game account, recovery link, purchased-credit balance, associated wallet and spending records, and local progress on that device. If a connection fails, purchases are disabled while deletion is pending; reconnect and use Retry connection to finish. You may explicitly start a new game after completion.

Deletion is irreversible. It does not delete your Apple ID, Game Center or Google account, and does not refund store purchases. Eligible permanent items can be restored to a new game account through the store. Deleted or spent consumable credits are not restored. Saved data on another device is not erased remotely by this action.

After deletion, we retain transaction hashes, product/type and revocation information with no player or account binding to prevent a previously used purchase from issuing credits again and to process later refunds. A hashed account replay barrier is scheduled to expire after one day, and an opaque deletion-completion receipt after seven days; database expiry processing may take additional time. These temporary records contain no name or contact details. They allow interrupted deletion requests to be completed safely.

For help with deletion, access, correction, portability, objection or other privacy rights available in your region, contact info@auravion.com. We may need information sufficient to verify that you control the relevant game account. Provider-held advertising or platform account data is also subject to the provider’s own controls and retention. You may contact your local data-protection authority if applicable.

Retention and security

We keep account and purchase records while needed to provide your wallet and ownership, resolve transactions and protect against duplicate purchases or abuse. Support information is kept while needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You can delete your game account as described above. We use access controls, encrypted connections and server-side store verification to protect information; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Strikefall is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. In the iOS privacy screen, ad-permission choices are for players aged 16 and older; younger players should choose “Don’t allow ads.” If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

We may update this policy as the game or services change. The current policy and revision date are available on this page.
