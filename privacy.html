Privacy Policy for Strikefall

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Strikefall is a mobile action game developed by Auravion. This policy explains how information is handled for game accounts, saved progress, purchases, advertising and support. Contact us at info@auravion.com or visit Strikefall support.

Game accounts and saved progress

You can play without registering an email address or creating a password. When online purchase services connect, Unity Authentication creates or reconnects a guest game account with a generated player identifier. We use this identifier to maintain your purchased-credit wallet and verified ownership.

Game progress, earned credits, settings and a cached copy of verified purchases are stored on your device. Purchased-credit balances and verified ownership are also maintained through our online services.

You may optionally connect Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android to protect and recover your purchased wallet. Unity receives the platform identifier and authentication proof needed to link that account. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

Recovery of a purchased wallet does not upload or restore all local mission progress or earned credits. Linking a platform account does not promise transfers between Android and iOS, or merge separate wallets.

Purchases, balances and refunds

Apple’s App Store or Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. Our server receives purchase proofs, product and transaction identifiers, store environment, purchase and refund status, and an account binding.

We verify this information with the relevant store to grant purchased items, restore eligible permanent purchases, prevent duplicate credits and apply refunds. We store purchased balances, permanent ownership, credit-spending operations, any refunded-credit shortfall and records used to prevent a transaction from being credited more than once.

These services run through Unity Gaming Services and our wallet backend hosted on Google Cloud. Store proofs are used for verification; we do not include them in ordinary application logs. Purchases and account recovery require an internet connection.

Refund requests are handled by the relevant store under its policies and applicable consumer rights. Restoring purchases does not issue previously granted consumable credits again. Eligible permanent items can be restored, while purchased-credit recovery uses the balance recorded in the associated wallet.

Advertising and device information

Strikefall uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Depending on the installed game version, advertising configuration and platform permissions, these services may process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device model, operating system, app version and language.
  • Ad views, interactions and related app activity.
  • Diagnostics, performance information and purchase-related signals.

This information is used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and operate advertising services. Personalisation depends on the version’s configuration and applicable permissions. Non-personalised advertising can still involve processing device information and other data for delivery, measurement and security.

Versions without the custom ad-choice screen

In versions without the custom personalised/basic/no-ad-permission screen, the game configures supported advertising integrations for non-personalised advertising. It sends a negative personalisation-consent signal and an opt-out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising before advertising services initialise.

These versions do not offer the former in-game “Don’t allow ads” setting. A choice saved through that older screen does not control ad availability after upgrading to a version without the screen. The new version applies its non-personalised configuration. Previously granting iOS tracking permission does not enable personalisation through this configuration.

Older iOS versions with the custom ad-choice screen

Some older iOS versions, including version 0.08 build 25, contain a custom screen offering personalised ads, basic ads or no ad permission. In those versions, personalised advertising additionally requires iOS tracking permission. Basic ads use a negative personalisation-consent signal and an opt-out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising.

If your installed version includes this screen, you can change its selection under Settings → Privacy & terms. Declining ad permission does not prevent gameplay or purchases, but advertising rewards require an available ad. After withdrawing ad permission in those versions, close and reopen the game to fully stop an advertising service already started during that session.

Device controls and No Ads purchases

You can manage available advertising and tracking controls in your device settings. Provider-specific privacy controls and deletion options may also be available through a privacy information or choices link within an advertisement.

The No Ads purchase removes scheduled interstitial advertisements. Optional rewarded advertisements remain available if you choose to watch them. A No Ads purchase does not delete information already held by an advertising provider.

For details about advertising data and provider controls, see Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy . Non-personalised settings are not a substitute for consent where applicable law requires it. This policy itself does not grant advertising or tracking consent.

Support and technical information

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or authentication codes.

Our service providers may process technical connection logs and diagnostics to keep authentication, purchases and the game operating securely.

Purposes and sharing

We use information to provide the game and purchases you request, maintain balances and ownership, offer optional account recovery, deliver and measure advertisements, respond to support requests, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability.

Where a legal basis is required, it depends on the processing and your location. Relevant bases may include performing the agreement to provide requested services, consent where required, legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention where permitted, and compliance with legal obligations.

We share information needed for these purposes with Unity, Apple or Google as the relevant platform, and Google Cloud as our backend hosting provider. Advertising providers may share information with advertising and measurement partners as described in their policies, subject to applicable settings, permissions and law.

Processing may take place outside your country. Providers describe their international-transfer safeguards in their privacy policies. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and service security.

Delete your game account and data

In the game, open Settings → Restore purchases → Delete account, read the warning and confirm account deletion. An internet connection is required.

This deletes the current game account, recovery link, purchased-credit balance, associated wallet and spending records, and local progress on that device. If a connection fails, purchases are disabled while deletion is pending. Reconnect and use Retry connection to finish. You may explicitly start a new game after deletion completes.

Deletion is irreversible. It does not delete your Apple ID, Game Center account or Google account, and it does not refund store purchases. Eligible permanent items can be restored to a new game account through the relevant store. Deleted or spent consumable credits are not restored. Saved data on another device is not erased remotely by this action.

After deletion, we retain transaction hashes, product/type and revocation information without a player or account binding. These records prevent a previously used purchase from issuing credits again and allow later refunds to be processed.

A hashed account replay barrier is scheduled to expire after one day, and an opaque deletion-completion receipt after seven days. Database expiry processing may take additional time. These temporary records contain no name or contact details and allow interrupted deletion requests to complete safely.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or portability of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact info@auravion.com for help exercising applicable rights or if you cannot access the in-game deletion controls. We may need information sufficient to verify that you control the relevant game account. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Provider-held advertising information and platform account data are also subject to the provider’s controls and retention. You may contact your local data-protection authority where applicable.

Retention and security

We keep account and purchase records while needed to provide your wallet and ownership, resolve transactions and protect against duplicate credits or abuse. Support information is kept while needed to handle your request and related follow-up. Account deletion and the records retained afterward are explained above.

We use access controls, encrypted connections and server-side store verification to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Strikefall is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Players must also meet any higher age requirement applicable to them or their store listing.

In older iOS versions that display the custom ad-choice screen, the ad-permission choices are intended for players aged 16 and older; younger players are instructed to choose “Don’t allow ads.” This screen is not present in versions using the non-personalised configuration described above.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact info@auravion.com so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.
